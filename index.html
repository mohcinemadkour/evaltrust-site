FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions before you talk to us

§01

What is EvalTrust?

EvalTrust is a sovereign AI evaluation and trust platform purpose-built for healthcare, finance, defense, and pharma — giving compliance and engineering teams continuous, defensible visibility into model accuracy, drift, hallucination risk, and policy adherence, before and after deployment.
§02

Which industries do you support?

We're built around the audit requirements of healthcare, financial services, defense & government, and pharma — organizations where an AI decision has to be reconstructable after the fact, not just accurate on average.
§03

How is EvalTrust deployed?

On-premises or in a private cloud — never shared SaaS. Data sovereignty is built into the platform from the ground up, so sensitive data stays inside your environment throughout evaluation and monitoring.
§04

How is our data handled?

Evaluation runs against your data inside your own on-prem or private-cloud deployment — it's not sent to a shared platform. We'll walk through specifics for anything sensitive, like PHI or PII, before any engagement starts.
§05

What's your pricing?

EvalTrust is currently onboarding pilot design partners in regulated industries, with pricing scoped per engagement based on deployment size and evaluation surface. Talk to our team for specifics.
