What happens in the pilot

A short, fixed plan. You put in about 3 hours a week; we do the rest.

WEEK 1

Agree on "good"

We run a workshop with your owners to set acceptance criteria, and build a golden dataset from 200–300 of your real cases.

WEEK 2

Connect and baseline

We test your staging endpoint and, if you have them, read your traces. We run a first baseline.

WEEK 3

Blind evaluation

Every answer is graded without knowing which version produced it. Each failure is traced to the agent that caused it.

WEEK 4

Gates and report

We set up a release gate for future changes and deliver the audit-ready report with a go/no-go recommendation.

What you get

Yours to keep after the pilot.

  • Signed acceptance criteria: what "ready" means, agreed by the people accountable for it.
  • A versioned golden dataset: your real cases with expected outcomes. You own it.
  • Blind evaluation results: per criterion and per agent, with root causes.
  • A release gate: the same tests, re-run on every model, prompt or agent change.
  • An audit-ready report for compliance, risk and leadership.

What we need from you

Light, and nothing in production.

  • A staging endpoint for your agent system, or trace exports if there's no endpoint yet.
  • 200–300 real cases, de-identified. We help you choose them.
  • 2–4 owners who sign off criteria: for example clinical, compliance, operations, engineering.
  • About 3 hours a week of their time, for 4 weeks.

Questions

Do you need access to production data?

No. The pilot uses a staging system and de-identified cases. When rules are strict, testing runs inside your own cloud account.

Do you replace our observability tool?

No. Keep Langfuse, LangSmith, Datadog or whatever you use. We read from it and add the parts those tools don't cover: agreed criteria, a golden dataset, blind grading, release gates and the report.

Which frameworks do you support?

Any. Endpoint testing only needs an API. Trace analysis works best with OpenTelemetry-compatible traces, which most agent frameworks can emit.

Who owns the golden dataset and results?

You do. They're delivered to you and stay useful long after the pilot.

What happens after the pilot?

Many teams keep the release gate and add monthly production monitoring. That's optional and decided at the end of week 4.
